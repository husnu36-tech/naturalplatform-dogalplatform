Welcome to Natural Platform! No Borders, No Censorship.

Hello Everyone,

Welcome to Natural Platform! We created this space because we are tired of arbitrary bans, user discrimination, and annoying rules on mainstream social networks. This is a completely free, independent, and open-source platform.

On our site, there are no approval queues, no hidden filters, and no corporate moderators who delete posts at their own discretion. Except for actions that directly threaten human safety (calls for physical violence, doxxing, or systematic harassment), anyone can freely write their thoughts on any topic.

Our core principle is simple: We do not ban or erase ideas we disagree with. If you disagree with a post, you can reply with your own solid arguments and debate it openly. Truth and real knowledge only emerge through free discussion.

Remember: Every post written here belongs entirely to its author. You own your data. You can completely delete your own content at any time without asking anyone.

🌿 OUR NATURE AND ENVIRONMENT COMMITMENT:
As this platform grows and transitions into an official non-profit association or foundation, all future donations and revenues (excluding technical server costs) will directly fund nature conservation and green initiatives. We unite human ideas to protect our planet.

The free podium is open, and the first discussion has started. From now on, the community's voice will shape this platform. The word is yours!
