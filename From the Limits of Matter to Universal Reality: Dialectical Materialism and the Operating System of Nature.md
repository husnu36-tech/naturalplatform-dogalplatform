Explanation of Foreign and Technical Terms Occurring in the Text

* **Utopia:** A fictional design of an ideal society that is impossible in reality or whose realization is longed for in the future.  
* **Communism:** A social system based on the common ownership of the means of production and a classless, moneyless, and stateless social order.  
* **Theorem:** A scientific proposition in mathematics or logic that is logically derived from accepted premises (axioms) and must be proven.  
* **Meningitis:** A dangerous inflammation of the membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord.  
* **Bronchitis:** An inflammation of the bronchial tubes that carry air into the lungs.  
* **Tuberculosis:** A microbial and infectious respiratory disease that usually affects the lungs.  
* **Aristocrat:** A member of the nobility, belonging to a privileged social class.  
* **Militant:** An organized person who actively fights for the success of an idea or a cause.  
* **Materialism:** A philosophical current asserting that the only substance in the universe is matter and that mental processes derive from matter.  
* **Feminist:** Relating to the mindset that demands that the rights of women and men should be socially, economically, and politically equal.  
* **Dialectics:** A method stating that everything in nature, society, and thought contains contradictions, and constantly changes and develops through the struggle of these contradictions.  
* **Illusion:** A deception or misperception in the mind that appears real although it is not.  
* **Entropy:** In physics and thermodynamics, the measure of the increase in disorder and randomness within a system.  
* **Bit:** In computer and information theory, the smallest processable digital unit of information (takes the value 0 or 1).  
* **Capitalism:** An economic and social system based on private ownership of the means of production and the pursuit of profit.  
* **Imperialism:** The endeavor of a state or a capital power to exploit other peoples and resources for its own benefit (colonialism).  
* **Monism:** A philosophical current stating that everything in the universe consists of a single fundamental substance (matter or energy).  
* **Pantheism:** A philosophical and religious view that considers the universe and God as one and the same, viewing nature itself as sacred.  
* **Substance (Töz):** The immutable essence that needs no other thing in order to exist.  
* **Superposition:** In quantum physics, the probability that a particle can exist in multiple states or positions simultaneously.  
* **Epistemology:** The branch of philosophy that studies the origin, nature, limits, and correctness of knowledge (theory of knowledge).
