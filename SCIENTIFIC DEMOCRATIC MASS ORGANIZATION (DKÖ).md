CHAPTER I: FOUNDING PROGRAM AND STRATEGIC GOALS

**Article 1: Character, Name, and Philosophical Identity of the Organization**

Historical and Scientific Foundation:** This structure draws its strength from the historical experiences of the natural communal history of humanity, the achievements of bourgeois democracy (rule of law), and the insights of modern science. The universal method of the organization is **Dialectical and Historical Materialism**.  
* **1.3. Purpose:** The elimination of psychological crises, isolation, alienation, and feudal/capitalist programming embedded in the human mind by the ruling system. The goal is to erase the old “subject/obedient” operating system in the brain and replace it with a **“free, scientific, and democratic”** operating system.

Through the **Union of Cells**, formed by the coming together of 3-person cells while preserving their own autonomy, a worldwide, borderless, broad democratic mass network shall be established to unite all of humanity on this free foundation.

The organization formulates itself into two independent but organically linked models based on the historical and cultural consciousness level of the masses:

* **Model A (Bourgeois-Democratic Line):** In countries of the Turkey type, where feudal codes, a culture of obedience, and a lack of rule of law are dominant, this model is **mandatory and essential**.
