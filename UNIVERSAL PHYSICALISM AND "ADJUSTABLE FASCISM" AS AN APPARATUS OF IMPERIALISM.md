UNIVERSAL PHYSICALISM AND "ADJUSTABLE FASCISM" AS AN APPARATUS OF IMPERIALISM

**Abstract (Makalenin Kısa Özeti):**

This paper demonstrates that fascism is not merely a historical regime (form of government) but a continuous, physical control mechanism of the imperialist-capitalist system, adjusted systematically according to the needs of systemic crises. Rooted in the principle that "everything is physics," human consciousness, psychology, workplace oppression (mobbing), and social movements are explained through their material (concrete) foundations. The study exposes state-controlled mechanisms of controlled opposition and scientifically proves that the physical violence of fascism can only be countered by organizing a physical, independent counter-force (barricade).

---

1\. INTRODUCTION: THE ACTORS IN THE SHOP WINDOW AND THE GLOBAL ILLUSION (YANILSAMA)

Mainstream political science (the generally established academic view) defines the rise of the far-right, such as the AfD (Alternative für Deutschland) in Germany or the RN (Rassemblement National) in France, as a temporary "deviation" or "sickness" of democracy. However, this approach is a fundamental illusion (yanılsama).

Political-economic (the relationship between economy and politics) evidence shows that fascism is not a phenomenon independent of imperialism (the global system of exploitation). Global capital recognizes the accumulated, justified anger of the masses over economic crises, inflation, and a bleak future. To prevent this anger from turning against the center of the system (large corporations and banks), it pushes structures like the AfD onto the stage as a **paratoner** (lightning rod / absorber of anger). The objective is to shift the target to refugees and minorities, thereby concealing the true culprit.

2\. SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATION: UNIVERSAL PHYSICALISM AND MATERIAL BASES

The most original theoretical contribution of this paper lies in linking the fundamental laws of nature with the socio-political (societal and political) sphere. According to **Universal Physicalism (the philosophy that everything is composed of physical matter)**, the universe consists exclusively of energy, atoms, and physical laws. Consequently, there is no abstract space that exists independently of human beings and society.

* **Psychology and Consciousness Are Physics:** Emotions such as fear, courage, love, hate, and submission are nothing more than electrical and neurochemical (chemical processes occurring in the brain) movements of atoms within the brain.  
* **The Virus and Antivirus Mechanism:** The imperialist system utilizes education, media, and propaganda to inject (introduce) a biological-digital virus (a software of obedience) into the brains of the masses. When an individual asks, "What is this filth in my head?" and recognizes reality, it triggers an **antivirus** (protective software) movement that physically restructures the neuronal connections (cellular connections) in the brain.  
* **Relations of Production (Maddi Taban):** As Marxist state theory emphasizes, every social phenomenon has a material basis and a specific relation of production (the reality of who produces and who exploits). The overproduction and exploitation crises of capitalism generate an immense thermodynamic (relating to the conversion of heat and energy) pressure within the system.

3\. "ADJUSTABLE LEVEL FASCISM" AND THE ANALOG VALVE MODEL

Klassik Marxist theories have often interpreted fascism as an "all-or-nothing" principle (either bourgeois democracy or open fascist dictatorship). This paper proves that fascism is not a digital switch (On/Off) but an **analog valve (a mechanism whose level can be increased or decreased fluidly)**. Fascism is permanently present worldwide, but it is calibrated (adjusted) at a different level in each country and era:

1. **Invisible Institutional Fascism:** In rich imperialist centers, fascism does not manifest as military boots on the streets, but through bureaucratic hurdles, institutional racism, and mechanisms of **mobbing** (psychological terror and harassment) injected deep into working life.  
2. **Mobbing as a Fascist Instrument:** Workplace mobbing is not an isolated (individual) problem of a "bad manager." It is an adjustable, micro-fascist (small-scale) tool systematically used to disarm the worker organizationally, isolate them, break their willpower, and prevent them from claiming their rights.

4\. STATE-CONTROLLED PSEUDO-OPPOSITION AND PRESSURE-RELEASE VALVES

The most sophisticated (complex and advanced) control mechanism of the system is that the opposition itself is designed by the state (in the sense of the deep state bureaucracy / the historical Gladio principle). The analysis of mass movements in Germany and globally provides clear evidence for this:

* **The Domestication (Zähmung) of the Systemic Left:** Today's structures that label themselves "Marxist" but fail to become mass movements or build barricades, as well as the peace and environmental movements, are not independent. The state bureaucracy finances these movements and provides them with space to absorb (suck up) the justified anger and kinetic energy (energy of motion) of the masses, converting them through permitted protests into harmless **pressure-release valves**.  
* **The Power of Visibility and Erasure:** As long as these movements remain within the state's boundaries, they are magnified by the media. However, the moment they touch the red lines of imperialism (the arms industry, military budgets), they are silenced and erased overnight by deep mechanisms. The transformation of former peace movements into today's warmongers is the most concrete proof of this social engineering (social manipulation).

Humanity must rip the adjustable valve of fascism out of its mind to survive.
